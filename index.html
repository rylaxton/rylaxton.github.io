About

I work on hardware: mechanical design, electrical systems, instrumentation, and the testing that tells you whether any of it actually worked. My projects run the full path from requirements and concept development through CAD, component selection, fabrication, and test, and the pages here are written to show that path rather than only the finished object.

I am studying Mechanical Engineering at Stanford, class of 2027. Most of my work has been hardware-facing: a welded steel thrust stand for a 300 lbf liquid engine that has been in service for cold-flow testing since early 2026, a jacketing retention clip designed and prototyped end to end during an internship at SkyCool Systems, and a sequence of projects through the Product Realization Lab covering casting, machining, sheet metal, and welding. At Cardinal Propulsion Lab I [am / was] Test and GSE Co-Lead, where the work is test setups, instrumentation, and ground electronics. I am looking for [internship / new grad] roles in experimental engineering and test systems, hardware development and prototyping, instrumentation and data acquisition, mechanical systems design and validation, or energy and propulsion.
